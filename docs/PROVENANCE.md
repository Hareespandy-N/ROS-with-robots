## Adding outside code or media later

Record the exact upstream URL/revision, author, applicable license, copied
file/asset, required notices, and modifications. Retain required notices and
check compatibility before publication. A public download or a GitHub link is
not a substitute for a reuse license. Link material when redistribution rights
are unknown. Do not import private robot files, credentials, factory maps,
logs, or customer material into this public research repository.

This provenance record documents what was included and the steps taken; it is
not a blanket legal clearance for future additions or a guarantee about every
jurisdiction's treatment of AI-assisted work.
